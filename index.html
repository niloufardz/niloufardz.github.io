About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electronic Systems at the Institut Jean Lamour, Université de Lorraine (Nancy, France), supervised by Prof. Philippe Poure and Prof. Slavisa Jovanovic. My thesis is on high-gain and fault-tolerant electronic interfaces for small-scale energy harvesting. I design the converter topologies, model them, simulate them and validate them on hardware prototypes. My defense is scheduled for 2 December 2026.

Before coming to France, I worked for four years in the photovoltaic laboratory of the University of Tehran. I have taught electrical circuits and power electronics since 2013, and I now chair the IEEE Student Branch of Université de Lorraine.

I am looking for postdoctoral and power electronics engineering positions.

Research interests

high-gain DC–DC converters single-switch & cascaded architectures energy harvesting · IoT nodes fault-tolerant converters fault diagnosis fault-tolerant control MPPT & voltage regulation AI / ML for power electronics sustainable electronics
